Overview

Kumarn: The Wandering Spirit is a dark and atmospheric horror adventure inspired by chilling Thai folklore. You play Kumarn, a childlike spirit once worshipped at a village shrine, now torn from his sacred home and cast into a haunting world of restless ghosts and cursed places. With your loyal zebra companion, journey through forgotten temples, haunted villages and forsaken graveyards to rediscover where you truly belong.
